7.2Depth measure

Term depth is exactly the same recursive measure as in Contender.v, with one new leaf: 

Fixpoint term_depth (t : term) : nat :=
  match t with
  | tVar x ⇒ S (Contender.nat_depth x)
  | tLam A B body ⇒
      S (max (max (Contender.type_depth A) (Contender.type_depth B))
             (term_depth body))
  | tApp t1 t2 ⇒ S (max (term_depth t1) (term_depth t2))
  | tO ⇒ 1
  | tS ⇒ 1
  | tNatRec R ⇒ S (Contender.type_depth R)
  | tGrow ⇒ 1
  end.

tGrow has depth 1, just like tO and tS: it is a constant. This is what lets the witness term tGrow (tS (embed t⋆)) stay within depth 44 when depth(t⋆) ≤ 42 (one tS application, one tGrow application, each adding 1).
